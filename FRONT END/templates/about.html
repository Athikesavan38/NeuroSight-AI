    About

    Brain hemorrhage, a critical medical condition characterized by bleeding within the brain tissue, poses significant diagnostic challenges and demands timely intervention. This study explores the efficacy of deep learning and machine learning approaches in detecting brain hemorrhages from medical imaging data. Leveraging convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and traditional machine learning algorithms, we aim to enhance the accuracy and reliability of hemorrhage detection. The research involves the collection and preprocessing of a comprehensive dataset of brain images, followed by the implementation and comparison of various models, including CNN, ResNet, MobileNet, and support vector machines (SVM).
